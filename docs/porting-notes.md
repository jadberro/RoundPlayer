### Playback "rock solid" — all daemon-side, tested by `tests/test_flows.py` (11 cases ALL OK) + `test_hifi_flow.py` (9) + `test_cast_flow.py`
Root causes of "pause then play something else → goes to a previous song": (1) yt-cast-receiver `doStop` → `stop_cast` popped `playback_history` and reloaded the previous track paused — fired on any phone disconnect; (2) HUD `_loadMedia` dropped a load arriving while another open was in flight (>300 ms for FLAC over HTTP); (3) two sources of truth (HUD optimistic play/pause + daemon adopting HUD state).
Now: `runtime_state["cast_track_id"]` set when `/api/play/{id}` carries `source:"cast"` (receiver sends it); `stop_cast` only acts if the active/caching track is cast-owned → `_enter_idle_locked()` (stop, remember `last_idle_track`), else no-op; any user pick clears ownership. HUD transport = ask only (`/api/control play|pause|seek`), no local player calls; `_pendingLoad` queue in `_loadMedia`; `post_hud_state` never changes `is_playing`; HUD reports ≥ every 2 s even when paused (keeps `hud_synced` fresh); end of track → `POST /api/hud/ended {track_id}` → repeat-one reload / `play_next_in_queue` (ignored if stale); idle → `POST /api/resume` reloads last track paused at its position. `_enter_idle_locked` used by idle/stop/stop_cast.
Autoplay: `similar.py` (Deezer related artists cached in `/data/hifi_related.json` 30 d; weights: same artist other album 3 / same album 1.5 / related 4 / ±7 yr 1 / same format family .5; ≤3 per album; widens exclusions when a small library runs dry). `play_next_in_queue` album end + repeat off + settings `autoplay_similar` (default true) → `_autoplay_pick` (no network under the lock; `_warm_related` prefetches on each Hi-Fi activation). Endpoints `GET /api/hifi/similar?track_id|artist&limit`, `POST /api/hifi/similar/play` (first now, rest queued).

### Boot audio (2026-10-18) — why it came up quiet/wrong and the fix
Three layers fought over the DAC's "Digital Playback Volume": udev `90-alsa-restore.rules` restored a stale `/var/lib/alsa/asound.state` (121 = −43 dB) *after* `dacpro.service` had applied 0 dB (card registers late), WirePlumber restored its remembered route volume (0.40) into the hardware mixer at every session start, and the HUD slider always started at 100 % regardless. Now: `51-dacpro.conf` sets `api.alsa.soft-mixer = true` (PipeWire never touches the DAC mixer; its own sink volume is software and forced to 1.0); `dacpro-ctl apply` waits for the card and ends with `alsactl store`; `services/91-dacpro.rules` re-applies via `systemd-run --on-active=2` when the card appears; the daemon owns the one volume: `settings.volume_pct` persisted by `/api/audio/volume`, `apply_boot_volume()` at startup applies DAC settings + saved volume and `pw_sinks_to_unity()`, telemetry `volume` is followed by the HUD slider (unless mid-drag). Verified after a reboot: Digital 207 (0 dB), deemphasis off, sink 1.00, daemon/HUD 100 %.
Settings → System → Clock: 12-hour (default, AM/PM in Bebas 64 beside the digits) / 24-hour (`clock_24h`). Pi helper scripts now live in `~/bin/` (hud_check.sh, hud_build_restart.sh, shot.sh) with `/tmp` symlinks — they survive reboots.

### YouTube streams instead of downloads (2026-10-19)
Casts / YouTube search / recently played no longer download + remux the whole video. `yt_stream.py` resolves with yt-dlp (`download=False`, ~2.5–3 s): video = `bestvideo[height<=1080][vcodec^=avc]+bestaudio[acodec^=mp4a]` merged into one `edl://!new_stream;…` URL (mpv streams both with range requests, seek works); audio-only (YouTube Music casts — receiver sends `music: video.client.key === 'YTMUSIC'` — or `settings.youtube_audio_only`) = `bestaudio` (opus). Cached per id until shortly before the URL's `expire=`. Daemon `set_active_track` → `_start_yt_stream` (prep_gen-guarded, thumbnail → cover.jpg centre-square + tint, `_note_played`); `_issue_cmd_locked` attaches `url`/`audio_only` to every load of a non-cached YouTube id and re-resolves in the background when the URL is unknown/expired (resume after hours, resync after restart). HUD `_loadMedia(url:)` opens the URL, sets mpv `start` for positioned starts; `audio_only` → art layout with the YouTube cover, kicker "YOUTUBE MUSIC · OPUS"; video kicker shows the real height. Lyrics also work for YouTube (`_fetch_lyrics_by_meta`, title cleaned of "(Official Video)" and "Artist - Song"). Locally cached videos still play from disk; `settings.youtube_keep_copy` downloads in the background without switching playback. Cast receiver waits 30 s max (was 90).
**yt-dlp had to be upgraded**: the distro 2025.04.30 only got 360p (format 18) in 10 s; PyPI `yt-dlp[default]` 2026.08.19 (+yt-dlp-ejs, node present) gets 1080p H.264 + AAC in 2.5 s. Installed `--user` (shadows /usr/lib/python3/dist-packages); install_pi.sh installs it; daemon `ytdlp_update_loop` upgrades weekly (effective at next restart). Verified live: cast at 30 s → playing 1080p from 30.0 s, synced, only cover.jpg on disk.

### Cast stop = standby, for real (2026-10-19)
What happened: stop_cast did go to standby, but `_enter_idle_locked` saved the cast video as `last_idle_track` (+ resume file), so the next touch on the clock (`/api/resume`, 4 s later in the log) reloaded it paused — looked like "it just paused". Now stop_cast clears `last_idle_track` and deletes the resume file (the video stays in Recently played); a touch opens the library. Also: the daemon issues a `stop` command at startup, so a HUD that outlived a daemon restart drops whatever it was still showing (seq reset re-arms the HUD). Video gradient now fades out with the controls (it used to dim the lower half permanently). test_flows #4 asserts /api/resume brings nothing back after a cast stop.
